## Analytical reading guide

Checkpoint 1 compares average early sales per store in dollars across four participation groups. Checkpoint 2 presents source cumulative m12 log-outcome profiles by brand, an observation-count matrix, and within-division program-vs-None differences. The second-checkpoint report follows the original method and findings from slides 17–31, with a table of follow-up evaluation priorities.

The [source manifest](source_manifest.json) records image hashes and confirms preservation of the original embedded assets and records the source, rendering method, and hash of the KPI slide preview. The [methodology](../../docs/methodology.md) defines the outcomes, comparison scales, and source and portfolio specifications. The [original notebooks](../../notebooks/README.md) preserve the analytical source code.
